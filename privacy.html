We do not use personal information for advertising, promotional campaigns, or marketing. This service is not used to send marketing or promotional text messages.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

This service is intended only for a small group of approved users and is not offered to the general public. It is designed for personal, conversational, and service-related messaging only.
