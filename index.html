Our Story

Restoring the Farm and Sharing the Experience

In 2025, we purchased a 100-acre farm in Petaluma, California. Many years had passed since the previous owners, who developed the farm in the 1980s, could care for it. We could still see remnants of their vision in the old barn, glass greenhouse, fruit orchard, and relics from the organic market garden. We decided to restore the farm, so that our family and farm team could share healthy food and beautiful experiences with the community.

How We Farm

Our Practices

We look forward to sharing our diverse, regenerative, and sustainable farm with you.

🌿

Organic

We work with California Certified Organic Farmers (CCOF) for our organic certifications. Our latest certifications can be found in the CCOF member directory.

🪱

Soil Health

We promote healthy soils by increasing organic matter, water infiltration, nutrient availability, and micro- and macrofauna.

💧

Local Resource Loops

We bring in compost from the community and use recycled water from Petaluma's Ellis Creek Water Recycling Facility.

🌤️

Carbon

We use solar panels to power our farm and reduce our carbon footprint, and our soil practices promote carbon sequestration.

🌻

Biodiversity

We look for diversity and synergy with our crops and livestock, promote insect diversity with our pollinators, and support wildlife corridors.

🐓

Animal Care

Our animals bring great joy to us and our children. We raise them with love in our pastures.
