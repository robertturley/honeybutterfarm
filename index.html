Our Story

Restoring the Farm and Sharing the Experience

In 2025, we purchased a 100-acre farm in Petaluma, California. Many years had passed since the previous owners, who developed the farm in the 1980s, could care for it. We could still see remnants of their vision in the old barn, glass greenhouse, fruit orchard, and relics from the organic market garden. We are restoring the farm so that our family and farm team can share healthy food and beautiful experiences with the community.

How We Farm

Our Practices

We look forward to sharing our diverse, regenerative, and sustainable farm with you.

🌿

Organic

We work with California Certified Organic Farmers (CCOF) for our organic certifications. Our latest certifications can be found in the CCOF member directory: Honeybutter Farm LLC.

🐓

Animal Care

Our animals bring great joy to us and our children. We honor their intrinsic characteristics as we raise them in our orchards and pastures.

🪱

Soil Health

Cover cropping, composting, mulching, and minimal tillage promote healthy soils and support micro- and macrofauna.

💧

Local Resource Loops

We bring in feed from local suppliers, compost from the community, and recycled water from Petaluma's Ellis Creek Water Recycling Facility.

🌤️

Carbon

We use solar panels to power our farm and reduce our carbon footprint, and our soil practices promote carbon sequestration.

🌻

Biodiversity

We look for diversity and synergy with our crops and livestock, promote insect diversity with our pollinators, and support wildlife corridors.
